# 06. Agile, Scrum and Jira

[Back to README](../README.md)

28 questions.

### Q1. What is a sprint?

A fixed timebox, usually one to four weeks, in which the team completes a set of work.

### Q2. What is a user story?

A short requirement from the user's view: "As a user, I want X so that Y".

### Q3. What are acceptance criteria?

Conditions a story must meet to be accepted. Testers use them to design tests.

### Q4. What is the definition of done (DoD)?

A shared checklist that says when work is complete, such as coded, tested, reviewed and documented.

### Q5. What is a product backlog?

A prioritized list of everything that may be needed in the product, owned by the Product Owner.

### Q6. What is a sprint backlog?

The set of items selected for the current sprint plus the plan to deliver them.

### Q7. What is a daily standup?

A short daily meeting where each member shares what they did, what they will do and any blockers.

### Q8. What is a sprint retrospective?

A meeting at the end of a sprint to discuss what went well, what did not, and what to improve.

### Q9. What is sprint review?

A meeting where the team demos finished work to stakeholders and gathers feedback.

### Q10. What are story points?

A relative unit used to estimate the effort and complexity of a story rather than hours.

### Q11. What is velocity?

The amount of work, usually story points, a team completes per sprint.

### Q12. What is a burndown chart?

A chart that shows remaining work against time in a sprint.

### Q13. What is Jira?

A popular tool for tracking issues, user stories, bugs and project progress, supporting Scrum and Kanban boards.

### Q14. What are common Jira issue types?

Epic, Story, Task, Bug and Sub-task.

### Q15. What is an epic?

A large body of work that is broken down into multiple stories.

### Q16. What is a Jira workflow?

The set of statuses and transitions an issue moves through, such as To Do, In Progress, In Testing and Done.

### Q17. What is a Jira dashboard?

A customizable page with gadgets that show project status, filters and reports.

### Q18. What is JQL?

Jira Query Language, used to search issues, for example: project = QA AND status = Open AND priority = High.

### Q19. How do you link a bug to a story in Jira?

Use the "Link issue" option with a relationship such as "is caused by" or "relates to" so traceability is clear.

### Q20. What is a Jira filter?

A saved JQL search that you can reuse and share.

### Q21. What is the role of a Product Owner?

Owns the backlog, sets priorities and represents the customer's needs.

### Q22. What is the role of a Scrum Master?

Facilitates Scrum events, removes blockers and helps the team follow the process.

### Q23. What is backlog grooming (refinement)?

Reviewing and clarifying backlog items so they are ready for upcoming sprints.

### Q24. What is a spike in Agile?

A short, timeboxed research task to answer a technical question or reduce risk.

### Q25. What is the three amigos meeting?

A conversation between business (PO/BA), development and testing to agree on a story's meaning and acceptance criteria before work starts.

### Q26. What is BDD?

Behavior-Driven Development: describing behavior in plain language (Given, When, Then) so business, developers and testers share understanding.

### Q27. What is TDD?

Test-Driven Development: write a failing test first, write code to pass it, then refactor.

### Q28. What is Gherkin?

A plain-text format using Given, When, Then keywords for writing BDD scenarios, used by tools like Cucumber.
