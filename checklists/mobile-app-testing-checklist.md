# Mobile App Testing Checklist

- [ ] Install, update and uninstall work
- [ ] App opens from a cold start and from the background
- [ ] Works in portrait and landscape
- [ ] Works on small and large screens and different OS versions
- [ ] Incoming call, SMS and notification do not break the app
- [ ] Switching between Wi-Fi, mobile data and offline is handled
- [ ] Low battery and low storage behavior
- [ ] Permissions (camera, location, storage) are requested at the right time and denial is handled
- [ ] Gestures: tap, swipe, pinch, long press
- [ ] Keyboard does not hide input fields
- [ ] Push notifications arrive and open the right screen
- [ ] App data stays after the app is closed and reopened
- [ ] No crashes or freezes during normal flows
